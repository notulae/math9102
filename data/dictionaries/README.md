# Data dictionaries

One page per dataset, generated from the data itself. For each
variable you get its storage type, how complete it is, and either
its range or its observed values.

**They do not tell you the level of measurement.** That is your
judgement to make and defend - see week 1 - and it is the decision
everything downstream rests on.
