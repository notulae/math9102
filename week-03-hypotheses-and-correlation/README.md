# Week 3 — Hypotheses and Correlation

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. State a hypothesis pair, choose a tail direction in advance, and fix α before
   looking at the data.
2. Define a *p*-value precisely, and say what it is not.
3. Distinguish Type I from Type II error, and explain what power is and what
   raises it.
4. Assess normality visually first, and explain why standardised skew ratios and
   normality tests cannot decide it at this module's sample sizes.
5. Name the three missing-data mechanisms, argue which one a dataset is facing,
   and choose and justify a treatment.
6. Screen for outliers by fence and by standardised score, and decide what a
   flagged value means.
7. Check linearity and homoscedasticity before computing a correlation.
8. Run, interpret and report a Pearson or Spearman correlation with its effect
   size and shared variance.
9. Say what a correlation does not license them to claim.
