# Workbook 1 — find what is wrong with this analysis

You have been given `analysis.ipynb`: a completed study of whether perceived
stress differs between respondents who have children and those who do not. It
runs. It has figures, a methods section and a results paragraph. It reads like
competent work.

**It contains three defects that materially affect what it concludes.**

Your job is to find them.

---

## What to write

For **each** of the three defects:

1. **Where it is.** Point at the cell or the paragraph.
2. **What is wrong.** Not "this looks odd" — say what the error *is*.
3. **Why it is wrong.** What principle does it break, or what does it
   contradict?
4. **The correction.** Fix it. If the fix changes a number, give the corrected
   number; if it changes the conclusion, say what the conclusion becomes.

Then, in a short paragraph:

5. **Rank the three** by how much each one changes what the analysis concludes,
   and justify the ranking. Several orderings are defensible; what matters is the
   reasoning about what an error does to a conclusion.

A page is plenty. A short answer that finds a defect beats a long one that
misses it.

Anything else genuinely wrong that you find is worth writing down too. Read the
notebook as an analyst, not as someone hunting for planted mistakes.

---

## Three things worth knowing before you start

**You may use AI on this.** Genuinely — try it. Paste the notebook into whatever
model you like and ask it to review the analysis. Then check what it tells you
against the notebook, because a good deal of it will be wrong in both
directions: things flagged that are fine, and things missed that are not.
Working out which is which is the exercise. It is also, increasingly, the job.

**Every defect here is real.** None was invented for this exercise. Each is an
error that turns up in real analyses, and survives because it **looks right**.
An error that looks wrong gets caught by anybody. The ones that matter are the
ones that read perfectly.

**Re-run things.** The notebook prints its own output. Some of what is wrong can
only be seen by reading a paragraph against the numbers in the cells above it,
and some only by re-computing something for yourself. A defect you can catch by
reading alone is the easy one.

---

## What you are not being asked

Not to rewrite the analysis. Not to improve its prose, its figures, or its
structure. Not to comment on anything that is merely a choice you would have
made differently — a defensible choice you disagree with is not a defect.

Fix what is *wrong*.
